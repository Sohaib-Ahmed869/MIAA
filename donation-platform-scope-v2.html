Donation Platform — Scope V2.0

Overview

This document is the finalised scope of work for the custom donation platform to be built for the Museum of Islamic Art Australia (MIAA). It supersedes the V1.0 proposal dated 22 June 2026 and incorporates all requirements discussed and confirmed in the discovery meeting held on 27 June 2026.

The platform is built directly into the existing MIAA website and extends the current CMS and admin portal — giving MIAA full ownership of the platform and its donor data with no separate system to manage.

The platform supports one-time and recurring giving, named donation products tied to specific museum causes, campaigns and supporter-led fundraising, and payments via Stripe and PayPal. It includes an optional donation upsell step during event registration, so supporters can add a contribution at the moment they purchase a ticket.

The Gala Dinner (25 July 2026) is included as the first active campaign and will have its own dedicated donation section on the Gala Dinner page, as well as visibility on the homepage hero — both controllable directly from the CMS without developer involvement.

The priority delivery target is the Gala Dinner on 25 July 2026. All core donation and event-linked features will be live and tested ahead of that date.

What's Included

The following capabilities are included in the build.

01
Donations
  • One-time donations with preset and custom amounts
  • Recurring donations — weekly, fortnightly, monthly, annual
  • Donor selects frequency and billing date at checkout
  • Installment plans — split a committed amount over multiple payments
  • Split donations across multiple causes or campaigns
  • Anonymous donation option — name kept private from public
  • All amounts in AUD
02
Payment Integrations
  • Stripe — Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Apple Pay, Google Pay
  • PayPal integration
  • Saved payment methods for recurring giving
  • Secure, PCI-compliant checkout
  • Card details handled by Stripe/PayPal — never stored on MIAA servers
03
Preset Donation Amounts & Named Causes
  • Admin configures preset donation amounts (e.g. $25, $50, $100, $250)
  • Each preset can be tied to a named cause or gallery area
  • Example causes: Faith Gallery & Prayer Hall, Children's Gallery, Entry Foyer, Touring Gallery & Function Room, Family Membership
  • Featured presets shown on the homepage
  • Custom amount entry always available alongside presets
  • Admin can add, edit, or remove products at any time
04
Campaigns & Appeals
  • Dedicated campaign pages with goal, progress bar & imagery
  • Gala Dinner 2026 campaign included and configured at launch
  • Multiple active campaigns running simultaneously
  • Admin creates and manages campaigns from the CMS
  • Campaign analytics — donations, payment methods, anonymous ratio
  • Campaigns can be attached to events so donors see the cause and progress while registering
  • Free events can carry an optional donation appeal alongside them
05 Confirmed in meeting
Donation Upsell at Event Checkout
  • Optional donation step presented during event registration checkout
  • Donor can add a donation on top of their ticket purchase in the same transaction
  • Donation frequency choice — one-time, weekly, monthly, or annual
  • Amount is freely selectable (preset options + custom entry)
  • Fully optional — donor can skip without affecting their registration
  • Donation is attributed to the event's linked campaign
  • Applies to paid and free events alike
06
Event Registration & Payments
  • Register and pay for events entirely within the MIAA website
  • No redirect to an external platform required for smaller events
  • Admin creates events in the CMS — banner, title, speaker, type, ticket price, capacity
  • Coupon/discount codes supported
  • Customisable registration questions (text, dropdown, file upload)
  • Email confirmation sent automatically on registration
  • All registration records stored in MIAA system
  • Free events supported (no payment required to register)
  • Note: QR code check-in scanning is not included in this scope
07
Peer-to-Peer Fundraising
  • Supporters create personal fundraising pages
  • Personal goals, story and progress tracking
  • Linked to MIAA campaigns
  • Admin review and approval of submitted pages
  • Visible under the parent campaign page
08
Receipts, Confirmations & Tax Statements
  • Automated email confirmation sent to donor for every payment
  • Admin notification email on every completed donation
  • Tax-deductible receipts (subject to MIAA/ISRA DGR status)*
  • Receipts downloadable from the donor portal
  • Annual tax statements per donor — generatable by admin
  • Receipts from Stripe/PayPal also forwarded to donor email
09 Confirmed in meeting
Audit Logging
  • All actions in the platform are logged with timestamp and user
  • Admin can view full donation history per transaction
  • Payment status visible — successful, pending, failed (insufficient funds, etc.)
  • Login and account activity tracked
  • Provides full accountability and dispute resolution capability
10
Donor User Portal
  • Donor account with secure login
  • Donation history and downloadable receipts
  • Manage recurring donations — update, pause, or request cancellation
  • Installment cancellation requires admin approval before processing
  • Manage saved payment methods
  • Create and manage own peer-to-peer fundraising pages
11
Admin Portal
  • Dashboard — donation totals, campaign performance, recent activity
  • Manage all donations and recurring subscriptions
  • Approve or reject donor cancellation requests for committed installments
  • Create and manage campaigns and appeals
  • Create and manage events and registration questions
  • Moderate and approve peer-to-peer fundraising pages
  • Generate annual tax statements per donor
  • Export donation records
  • Newsletter and volunteer management
  • Dedicated donation admin portal available for a donations-only team member
12
CRM Sync (Brevo)
  • Donors automatically synced into Brevo on first donation
  • Newsletter sign-ups synced into Brevo
  • Deduplicated by email address
  • Donation event data tagged for segmentation
13 Confirmed in meeting
Website Integration & CMS-Controlled Placement
  • Built into the existing MIAA website — no separate platform
  • On-brand and fully mobile responsive
  • Hosted on MIAA's existing infrastructure
  • Donation widget on the homepage hero section — each slide controllable independently from the CMS
  • Dedicated donation section on the Gala Dinner event page
  • Donation section embeddable on any current or future page via the CMS
  • Admin controls hero slides, images, and donation calls-to-action without developer involvement
  • New event pages can be created from preset templates and customised (colours, images, layout) from the CMS

* Tax-deductible receipting is configured subject to MIAA / ISRA's deductible-gift-recipient (DGR) status and the receipt details MIAA provides. Think Studio will configure the receipt template once DGR details are confirmed.

How It's Built

The platform extends MIAA's existing website rather than introducing a separate system, keeping a single brand, codebase, and hosting footprint. All donation and event functionality is managed from the same admin portal the team already uses.

Front End

  • Built directly into the existing MIAA website
  • Fully responsive — desktop, tablet, and mobile
  • MIAA brand colours, typography, and logo throughout
  • Donation widget embeddable on any page via the CMS

Back End

  • Extends the current MIAA backend and admin CMS
  • Donations, campaigns, events, and donor accounts
  • Auto-scaling infrastructure — handles traffic spikes (e.g. Gala launch)
  • Server capacity adapts to the number of active users

Payments

  • Stripe (cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay) and PayPal
  • Card details handled entirely by the payment gateway
  • Not stored on or transmitted through MIAA servers
  • Webhooks used to confirm payment status in real time

Security

  • Served over HTTPS
  • Admin and donor areas behind secure authentication
  • Full audit log of all actions and payment events
  • PCI-compliant payment flow

Delivery Timeline

The primary milestone is the Gala Dinner on 25 July 2026. All core donation, event payment, and donation-upsell features will be live and client-tested ahead of that date. Remaining features (peer-to-peer, advanced admin reporting) will follow in the weeks after.

1
Week of 27 June 2026
Scope sign-off & development kickoff
Final scope confirmed. Development of donation core, Stripe/PayPal integration, and event registration begins.
2
Week of 7 July 2026
Internal build — core features complete
Donations, recurring payments, preset amounts, campaigns, event checkout with donation upsell, homepage hero widget, and Gala Dinner page section built and internally tested.
3
Week of 14 July 2026
Client UAT (User Acceptance Testing)
MIAA team tests all features on a staging environment. Feedback incorporated. Gala Dinner campaign configured and reviewed.
★
25 July 2026
Gala Dinner — platform live
Donation platform fully live on the MIAA website. Gala Dinner campaign active. Event payments and donation upsell operational.
4
August 2026
Phase 2 — remaining features
Peer-to-peer fundraising pages, advanced admin reporting, annual tax statement generation, and any post-Gala feedback items.

Exact dates will be confirmed once development commences. Timeline assumes timely provision of brand assets, Stripe/PayPal account credentials, and DGR receipt details by MIAA.
